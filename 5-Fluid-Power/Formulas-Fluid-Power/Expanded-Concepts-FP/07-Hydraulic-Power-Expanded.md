# Hydraulic Power

## Formula
`HP = (PSI × GPM) ÷ 1714`

## Detailed Explanation
Hydraulic power combines pressure and flow into a single measurement of work capability.

## Practice Problems
1. Find horsepower at 1,500 psi and 10 gpm.
2. Find horsepower at 2,500 psi and 25 gpm.
3. Can power be produced with pressure but no flow?

## Answers
1. 8.75 hp
2. 36.5 hp
3. No.
