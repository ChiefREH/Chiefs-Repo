# Pressure, Force, and Area

## Formulas
`P = F ÷ A`

`F = P × A`

`A = F ÷ P`

## Detailed Explanation
These are the most important formulas in fluid power. Force increases when pressure increases or when area increases.

## Worked Example
Pressure = 1,500 psi
Area = 8 in²

`F = 1,500 × 8 = 12,000 lb`

## Troubleshooting Use
Low cylinder force may be caused by:
- Low system pressure
- Incorrect cylinder bore size
- Internal leakage

## Practice Problems
1. Find force developed by 2,000 psi acting on 12 in².
2. A cylinder develops 15,000 lb at 1,500 psi. Find piston area.
3. Calculate pressure required to produce 8,000 lb using a 5 in² piston.

## Answers
1. 24,000 lb
2. 10 in²
3. 1,600 psi
