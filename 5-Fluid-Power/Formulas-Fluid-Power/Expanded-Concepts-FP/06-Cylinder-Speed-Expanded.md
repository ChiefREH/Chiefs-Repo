# Cylinder Speed

## Formula
`V = Q ÷ A`

## Detailed Explanation
Cylinder velocity increases as flow increases and decreases as piston area increases.

## Practice Problems
1. Find speed when flow is 20 and area is 5.
2. What happens to speed if area doubles?
3. What happens to speed if flow doubles?

## Answers
1. 4 units/sec
2. Speed decreases by half.
3. Speed doubles.
