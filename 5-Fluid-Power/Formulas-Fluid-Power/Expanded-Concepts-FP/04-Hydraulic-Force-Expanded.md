# Hydraulic Force

## Formula
`F = P × A`

## Detailed Explanation
Hydraulic cylinders convert fluid pressure into usable mechanical force.

## Example
Pressure = 2,500 psi
Area = 10 in²

Force = 25,000 lb

## Practice Problems
1. Calculate force from 3,000 psi and 15 in².
2. Calculate force from 1,200 psi and 6 in².
3. What happens to force if pressure doubles?

## Answers
1. 45,000 lb
2. 7,200 lb
3. Force doubles.
