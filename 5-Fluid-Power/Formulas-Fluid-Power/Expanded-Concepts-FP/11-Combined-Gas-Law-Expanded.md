# Combined Gas Law

## Formula
`(P1 × V1)/T1 = (P2 × V2)/T2`

## Detailed Explanation
The combined gas law accounts for simultaneous changes in pressure, temperature, and volume.

## Practice Problems
1. What pressure units must be used?
2. What temperature scale must be used?
3. Which three variables are related by this law?

## Answers
1. Absolute pressure.
2. Absolute temperature.
3. Pressure, volume, and temperature.
