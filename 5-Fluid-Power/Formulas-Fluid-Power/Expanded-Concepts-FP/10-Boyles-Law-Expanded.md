# Boyle's Law

## Formula
`P1 × V1 = P2 × V2`

## Detailed Explanation
As pressure increases, volume decreases proportionally if temperature remains constant.

## Practice Problems
1. Air occupies 8 ft³ at 20 psia. Find volume at 40 psia.
2. Air volume is reduced from 10 ft³ to 5 ft³. What happens to pressure?
3. Name two devices that use Boyle's Law.

## Answers
1. 4 ft³
2. Pressure doubles.
3. Compressors and accumulators.
