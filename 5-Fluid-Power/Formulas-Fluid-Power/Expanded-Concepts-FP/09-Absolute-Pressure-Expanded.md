# Absolute Pressure

## Formula
`Pabs = Pgauge + 14.7`

## Detailed Explanation
Gas-law calculations require pressure referenced to a perfect vacuum.

## Practice Problems
1. Convert 150 psig to psia.
2. Convert 50 psig to psia.
3. Why is absolute pressure required for gas laws?

## Answers
1. 164.7 psia
2. 64.7 psia
3. Gas behavior is measured from absolute zero pressure.
