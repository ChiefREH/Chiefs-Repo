# Pneumatic Force

## Formula
`F = P × A`

## Detailed Explanation
Air cylinders use the same force relationship as hydraulic cylinders but with lower operating pressures.

## Practice Problems
1. Calculate force from 100 psi and 8 in².
2. Calculate force from 80 psi and 12 in².
3. Why are pneumatic systems generally less rigid?

## Answers
1. 800 lb
2. 960 lb
3. Air is compressible.
