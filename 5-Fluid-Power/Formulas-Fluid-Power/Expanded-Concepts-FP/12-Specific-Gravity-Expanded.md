# Specific Gravity

## Formula
`SG = Densityfluid ÷ Densitywater`

## Detailed Explanation
Specific gravity compares a fluid's density to water.

## Applications
- Fluid selection
- Reservoir calculations
- Weight calculations

## Practice Problems
1. Is an oil with SG 0.90 heavier or lighter than water?
2. What is the SG of water?
3. Which is denser, a fluid with SG 1.2 or SG 0.8?

## Answers
1. Lighter.
2. 1.0
3. SG 1.2
