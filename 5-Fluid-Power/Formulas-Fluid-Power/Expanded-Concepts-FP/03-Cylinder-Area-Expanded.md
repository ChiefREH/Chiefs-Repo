# Cylinder Area

## Formula
`A = (π × D²) ÷ 4`

## Detailed Explanation
Cylinder area determines both force capability and actuator speed. Larger cylinders produce more force but require more flow to maintain speed.

## Worked Example
Diameter = 5 in

`A = (3.1416 × 25) ÷ 4`

`A = 19.63 in²`

## Practice Problems
1. Calculate area of a 3-inch bore cylinder.
2. Calculate area of a 6-inch bore cylinder.
3. Which cylinder produces more force at equal pressure, a 3-inch or 5-inch cylinder?

## Answers
1. 7.07 in²
2. 28.27 in²
3. 5-inch cylinder
