# Flow Rate

## Formula
`Q = A × V`

## Detailed Explanation
Flow rate is the volume of fluid moving through a hydraulic system over time. Flow controls actuator speed.

## Practice Problems
1. If area is 5 in² and velocity is 10 in/sec, find flow.
2. If velocity doubles, what happens to flow?
3. Does pressure or flow primarily determine speed?

## Answers
1. 50 in³/sec
2. Flow doubles.
3. Flow.
