# Hydraulic Force

## Formula
`F = P × A`

## Variables
- F = Force (lb)
- P = Pressure (psi)
- A = Area (in²)

## What It Means
Hydraulic force is created when pressure acts on a piston area.

## Example
A 12.57 in² piston operating at 2,000 psi produces:

`F = 2,000 × 12.57 = 25,140 lb`

## Practical Application
Used when sizing cylinders for presses, lifts, clamps, and industrial machinery.
