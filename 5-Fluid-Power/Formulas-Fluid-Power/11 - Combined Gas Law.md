# Combined Gas Law

## Formula
`(P₁ × V₁) ÷ T₁ = (P₂ × V₂) ÷ T₂`

## Variables
- P = Absolute Pressure
- V = Volume
- T = Absolute Temperature

## What It Means
This law combines pressure, volume, and temperature into a single relationship.

## Requirements
Pressure must be absolute pressure and temperature must be absolute temperature (°R or K).

## Applications
Used whenever a gas changes pressure, volume, and temperature simultaneously.
