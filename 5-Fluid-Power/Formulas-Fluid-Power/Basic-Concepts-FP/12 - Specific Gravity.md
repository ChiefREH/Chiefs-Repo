# Specific Gravity

## Formula
`SG = Densityfluid ÷ Densitywater`

## Variables
- SG = Specific Gravity

## What It Means
Specific gravity compares the density of a fluid to the density of water.

## Examples
- Water = 1.0
- Typical hydraulic oil = 0.85 to 0.95

## Why It Matters
Specific gravity affects fluid weight, reservoir sizing, pump inlet conditions, and certain pressure calculations.

## Key Concept
A fluid with an SG less than 1.0 is lighter than water.
