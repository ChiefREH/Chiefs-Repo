# Hydraulic Power

## Formula
`HP = (PSI × GPM) ÷ 1714`

## Variables
- PSI = Pressure
- GPM = Flow Rate
- HP = Hydraulic Horsepower

## What It Means
Hydraulic power is a measure of work being performed by a hydraulic system.

## Example
At 2,000 psi and 20 gpm:

`HP = (2,000 × 20) ÷ 1714 = 23.3 hp`

## Key Concept
Pressure supplies force and flow supplies speed. Both are required to produce power.
