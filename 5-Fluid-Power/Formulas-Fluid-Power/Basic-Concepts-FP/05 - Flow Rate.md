# Flow Rate

## Formula
`Q = A × V`

## Variables
- Q = Flow Rate
- A = Area
- V = Velocity

## What It Means
Flow rate is the volume of fluid moving through a system over time.

## Why It Matters
Flow determines how quickly a hydraulic actuator moves. Unlike pressure, flow does not directly create force.

## Key Concept
Pressure creates force. Flow creates motion.
