# Cylinder Area

## Formula
`A = (π × D²) ÷ 4`

## Variables
- A = Piston Area (in²)
- D = Bore Diameter (in)

## What It Means
The piston face is circular. This equation calculates the effective area that hydraulic pressure acts against.

## Example
For a 4-inch bore cylinder:

`A = (3.1416 × 4²) ÷ 4 = 12.57 in²`

## Why It Matters
Cylinder area directly determines force output and actuator speed.
