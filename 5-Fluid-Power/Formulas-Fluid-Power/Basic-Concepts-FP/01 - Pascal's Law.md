# Pascal's Law

## Formula / Principle
Pressure applied to a confined fluid is transmitted equally in all directions.

## What It Means
Pascal's Law is the fundamental principle behind hydraulic systems. When pressure is created by a pump, that pressure exists throughout the hydraulic circuit. The pressure at one point in a closed fluid system is transmitted equally to every other point in the system.

## Why It Matters
This allows a small input force to be converted into a much larger output force when applied to a larger area.

## Example
If 100 psi is applied to a hydraulic system, every square inch of surface area exposed to the fluid experiences 100 psi.

## Key Concept
Pressure is the same throughout a confined fluid, but force changes with area.
