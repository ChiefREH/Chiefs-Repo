# Boyle's Law

## Formula
`P₁ × V₁ = P₂ × V₂`

## What It Means
For a fixed amount of gas at constant temperature, pressure and volume are inversely proportional.

## Example
If air at 14.7 psia occupies 10 ft³ and is compressed to 5 ft³, pressure doubles to approximately 29.4 psia.

## Applications
Used in compressors, accumulators, air receivers, and pneumatic systems.
