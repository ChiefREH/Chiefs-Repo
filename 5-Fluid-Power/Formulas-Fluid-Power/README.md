# Fluid Power Essentials
## One-Page Formula Sheet

### Common Hydraulic Units

| Quantity | Unit |
|-----------|------|
| Pressure | psi |
| Flow Rate | gpm |
| Area | in² |
| Force | lb |
| Power | hp |

---

# Pascal's Law

**Pressure applied to a confined fluid is transmitted equally in all directions.**

### Key Concept

- Pressure is transmitted equally throughout a hydraulic system.
- Pressure creates force.
- Flow creates motion.

---

# Pressure, Force, and Area

### Calculate Pressure

`P = F ÷ A`

### Calculate Force

`F = P × A`

### Calculate Area

`A = F ÷ P`

Where:

- `P` = Pressure (psi)
- `F` = Force (lb)
- `A` = Area (in²)

### Remember

> **Pressure creates force.**

---

# Cylinder Area

### Piston Area

`A = (π × D²) ÷ 4`

Where:

- `A` = Piston Area (in²)
- `D` = Bore Diameter (in)

### Common Cylinder Areas

| Bore Diameter | Area |
|---------------|-------|
| 2 in | 3.14 in² |
| 3 in | 7.07 in² |
| 4 in | 12.57 in² |
| 5 in | 19.63 in² |
| 6 in | 28.27 in² |

---

# Hydraulic Force

### Force Produced by a Cylinder

`F = P × A`

Example:

```text
2,000 psi × 12.57 in² = 25,140 lb
```

### Remember

- Higher pressure = More force
- Larger piston area = More force

---

# Flow Rate

### Flow Equation

`Q = A × V`

Where:

- `Q` = Flow Rate
- `A` = Area
- `V` = Velocity

### Remember

> **Flow creates motion.**

---

# Cylinder Speed

### Velocity Equation

`V = Q ÷ A`

Where:

- `V` = Velocity
- `Q` = Flow Rate
- `A` = Area

### Remember

| Change | Result |
|----------|----------|
| Increase Flow | Increase Speed |
| Increase Area | Decrease Speed |

---

# Hydraulic Power

### Horsepower Equation

`HP = (PSI × GPM) ÷ 1714`

Where:

- `HP` = Horsepower
- `PSI` = Pressure
- `GPM` = Flow Rate

### Remember

> **Pressure creates force.**
>
> **Flow creates speed.**
>
> **Pressure + Flow create power.**

---

# Pneumatic Force

### Air Cylinder Force

`F = P × A`

Same relationship used for hydraulic cylinders.

### Remember

- Hydraulics use liquid.
- Pneumatics use compressed air.
- Air is compressible.
- Hydraulic oil is essentially incompressible.

---

# Absolute Pressure

### Conversion Formula

`Pabs = Pgauge + 14.7`

Where:

- `Pabs` = Absolute Pressure (psia)
- `Pgauge` = Gauge Pressure (psig)

Example:

```text
100 psig + 14.7 = 114.7 psia
```

### Remember

> **Gas law calculations require absolute pressure.**

---

# Boyle's Law

### Formula

`P₁ × V₁ = P₂ × V₂`

### Relationship

| Pressure | Volume |
|----------|---------|
| Increase | Decrease |
| Decrease | Increase |

### Remember

> **Pressure and volume are inversely proportional.**

---

# Combined Gas Law

### Formula

`(P₁ × V₁) ÷ T₁ = (P₂ × V₂) ÷ T₂`

Where:

- `P` = Absolute Pressure
- `V` = Volume
- `T` = Absolute Temperature

### Temperature Requirements

Use:

- Kelvin (K)
- Rankine (°R)

Do NOT use:

- Fahrenheit (°F)
- Celsius (°C)

---

# Specific Gravity

### Formula

`SG = Densityfluid ÷ Densitywater`

### Reference Values

| Fluid | Specific Gravity |
|---------|---------|
| Water | 1.0 |
| Hydraulic Oil | 0.85 - 0.95 |

### Remember

- `SG < 1.0` → Lighter than water
- `SG > 1.0` → Heavier than water

---

# Fluid Power Fundamentals

### Pumps Create Flow

> Pumps create flow, not pressure.

---

### Pressure Develops from Resistance

> Flow + Resistance = Pressure

---

### Hydraulic Systems

- Use liquid
- High force capability
- Precise motion
- Typical pressure: 500-5000+ psi

---

### Pneumatic Systems

- Use compressed air
- Fast operation
- Less precise
- Typical pressure: 80-120 psi
