# Fluid Power Formula Reference

## Pressure, Force & Area

| Solve For | Formula |
|-----------|---------|
| Force (F) | F = P × A |
| Pressure (P) | P = F / A |
| Area (A) | A = F / P |

Where:

- F = Force (lb)
- P = Pressure (psi)
- A = Area (in²)

---

## Cylinder Areas

### Bore Area

A = (π × D²) ÷ 4

### Rod Area

A = (π × d²) ÷ 4

### Annulus (Rod-Side) Area

A = [π × (D² - d²)] ÷ 4

Where:

- D = Bore diameter (in.)
- d = Rod diameter (in.)

---

## Hydraulic Cylinder Force

### Extension Force

Force = Pressure × Bore Area

F = P × Ab

### Retraction Force

Force = Pressure × Annulus Area

F = P × Aa

---

## Flow & Cylinder Speed

### Flow Rate

Q = A × V

### Cylinder Velocity

V = (231 × GPM) ÷ A

### Required Flow

GPM = (A × V) ÷ 231

Where:

- Q = Flow
- A = Area (in²)
- V = Velocity (in./min)

---

## Hydraulic Power

### Hydraulic Horsepower

HP = (PSI × GPM) ÷ 1714

### Motor Horsepower Required

HP = (PSI × GPM) ÷ (1714 × Efficiency)

---

## Pump Formulas

### Pump Flow

GPM = (Displacement × RPM) ÷ 231

### Pump Displacement

Displacement = (231 × GPM) ÷ RPM

Where:

- Displacement = in³/rev

---

## Hydraulic Motor Formulas

### Motor Speed

RPM = (231 × GPM) ÷ Displacement

### Motor Torque

Torque (lb-in) = (PSI × Displacement) ÷ 75.4

---

## Pneumatic Cylinder Force

### Theoretical Force

F = P × A

### Practical Force (≈85% Efficiency)

F = P × A × 0.85

---

## Accumulator Formula

### Boyle's Law

P1 × V1 = P2 × V2

---

## Hose Velocity

Velocity (ft/sec) = (0.321 × GPM) ÷ d²

Where:

- d = Hose I.D. (in.)

Recommended Velocities:

| Line Type | Velocity |
|------------|------------|
| Suction | 2–4 ft/sec |
| Return | 5–10 ft/sec |
| Pressure | 10–20 ft/sec |

---

## Efficiency

### Volumetric Efficiency

Efficiency (%) =
(Actual Flow ÷ Theoretical Flow) × 100

### Mechanical Efficiency

Efficiency (%) =
(Actual Torque ÷ Theoretical Torque) × 100

### Overall Efficiency

Overall = Volumetric × Mechanical
