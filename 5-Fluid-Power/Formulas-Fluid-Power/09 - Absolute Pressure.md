# Absolute Pressure

## Formula
`Pabs = Pgauge + 14.7`

## Variables
- Pabs = Absolute Pressure (psia)
- Pgauge = Gauge Pressure (psig)

## What It Means
Gauge pressure measures pressure above atmospheric pressure. Absolute pressure includes atmospheric pressure.

## Example
A reading of 100 psig equals:

`100 + 14.7 = 114.7 psia`

## Key Concept
All gas law calculations require absolute pressure.
