# Pneumatic Force

## Formula
`F = P × A`

## Variables
- F = Force
- P = Air Pressure
- A = Piston Area

## What It Means
Compressed air follows the same basic force relationship as hydraulics.

## Example
A 5 in² piston at 100 psi produces:

`F = 100 × 5 = 500 lb`

## Key Difference
Air is compressible, so pneumatic systems are generally less rigid and less precise than hydraulic systems.
