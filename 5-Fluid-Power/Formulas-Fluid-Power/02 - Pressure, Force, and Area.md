# Pressure, Force, and Area

## Formulas
`P = F ÷ A`

`F = P × A`

`A = F ÷ P`

## Variables
- P = Pressure (psi)
- F = Force (lb)
- A = Area (in²)

## What It Means
Pressure is force distributed over an area. These three equations are the foundation of almost every hydraulic and pneumatic calculation.

## Example
A cylinder with 10 in² of piston area operating at 1,000 psi produces:

`F = 1,000 × 10 = 10,000 lb`

## Key Concept
Increasing either pressure or area increases output force.
