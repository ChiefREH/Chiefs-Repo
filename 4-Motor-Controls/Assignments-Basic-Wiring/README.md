## FUSES

- **DO NOT** insert or remove fuses with your fingers!
    - Always use fuse pullers

### THE UPPER SECTION IS ALWAYS ENERGIZED!

![Shock Hazard](Assignment-Motors-Basic-Images/Main-Dosconnect-Danger.jpg)



## WIRE NUTS

- No wire nuts are permitted to be used as wire junctions
    - Find a proper length of wire to make your connections
    - The only place wire nuts will be used is on the 9-wire motor connections (see below)

![Wire Nuts](Assignment-Motors-Basic-Images/Motor-Connections.jpg)

## LADDER DIAGRAMS

When wiring from a ladder diagram:
- Wire from top to bottom
- Wire from left to right
