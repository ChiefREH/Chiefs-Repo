# INDT Career Fair Reflection Report

## Purpose

The INDT Career Fair provides an opportunity to explore careers, learn about employers, and develop professional networking skills by speaking directly with industry representatives.

---

# Career Fair Participation

Attend the **INDT Career Fair** and spend approximately **2 hours** at the event.

During the Career Fair:

- Visit all vendors and presenters.
- Spend approximately **5 minutes** speaking with each organization.
- Introduce yourself professionally.
- Ask meaningful questions about careers, internships, skills, certifications, and employment opportunities.
- Take notes during or immediately after each conversation.
- Collect available materials such as brochures, flyers, business cards, and handouts.
- Keep collected materials organized for use in your report.

---

# Suggested Questions

Consider asking questions such as:

- What careers are available in your organization?
- What skills and qualifications do you look for in applicants?
- Do you offer internships or entry-level opportunities?
- What advice would you give students entering this field?
- What makes a strong candidate for your organization?

You are encouraged to develop additional questions based on your interests and each organization's area of work.

---

# Written Report

After attending the Career Fair, prepare a written report that summarizes your experience.

Your report must:

- Be at least **750 words**.
- Identify the vendors and presenters you interacted with.
- Summarize what you learned from each conversation.
- Describe the questions you asked and the responses you received.
- Highlight organizations, opportunities, or information that you found especially valuable.
- Explain how the experience influenced your career interests or goals.
- Include images of materials collected from vendors or presenters.

Use clear headings and professional formatting throughout the report.

---

# Submission Requirements

Submit your report through **Canvas** in one of the following formats:

- Microsoft Word (`.doc` or `.docx`)
- PDF (`.pdf`)

Before submitting, verify that:

- The file opens correctly.
- All images are visible and readable.
- The report meets the minimum word count requirement.

---

# Grading Criteria

Reports will be evaluated on:

- Attendance and participation
- Engagement with vendors and presenters
- Quality of questions and notes
- Depth of reflection and analysis
- Organization and professionalism
- Writing quality
- Inclusion of supporting images and documentation

---

# After Submission

Be prepared to answer follow-up questions regarding your Career Fair experience, vendor interactions, collected materials, and written report.
