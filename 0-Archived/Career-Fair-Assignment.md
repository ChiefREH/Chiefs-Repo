Purpose

The Career Fair assignment gives you an opportunity to learn about employers, career opportunities, and professional expectations by speaking directly with representatives.

What You Need to Do

Attend the Spring Career Fair for approximately 2 hours.

During the career fair:

Visit all vendors and presenters.
Spend approximately 5 minutes speaking with each vendor or presenter.
Introduce yourself and ask questions that help you learn about the organization, career opportunities, internships, or other relevant topics.
Take notes during or immediately after each conversation.
Collect available presentation materials from each vendor or presenter. Examples include brochures, business cards, flyers, or other handouts.
Keep your materials organized so you can identify which vendor or presenter provided each item.
Suggested Questions

You may ask questions such as:

What types of careers are available in your organization?
What skills or qualifications do you look for in applicants?
Do you offer internships, job-shadowing, or entry-level opportunities?
What advice would you give students preparing for a career in this field?
What makes a strong candidate for your organization?

You are encouraged to develop additional questions based on each organization's area of work.

Written Report

After attending the career fair, prepare a written report describing your experience.

Your report must:

Be at least 750 words.
Identify the vendors and presenters you interacted with.
Summarize the information you learned from your conversations.
Describe the questions you asked and the responses you received.
Identify information, opportunities, or organizations that you found particularly interesting or useful.
Include images of the presentation materials collected from each vendor or presenter.
Explain what you learned from participating in the career fair.

Organize your report with clear headings so that each section is easy to locate.

Submission Requirements

Submit your completed report through Canvas.

Accepted file formats:

Microsoft Word document (.doc or .docx)
PDF (.pdf)

Make sure that your submitted file opens correctly and that all images and text are visible and readable.

After Submission

Be prepared to answer follow-up questions about your career fair experience and written report after the instructor has reviewed your submission.
